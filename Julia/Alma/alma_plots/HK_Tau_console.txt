  Coherence across rings (a vortex sits at one radius; a centre / inclination / PA error shows at every ring with the same orientation):
    fewer than 2 rings with a significant amplitude: no coherence test.

  Candidate arcs / clumps (beam-smoothed residual above 4.0 sigma of the smoothed residual, >= 1 beam of area, outside the 39 au exclusion zone):
    none

  How to read this: a single significant positive arc covering a limited azimuth range at a ring radius (span of tens of degrees,
  contrast tens of percent) is the signature of a dust trap such as a vortex. A +/- pair 180 deg apart (in the residual image, not the ring-averaged amplitudes)
  is what a small centre or inclination error produces.
  The noise estimate for the arcs is the robust scatter of the smoothed residual itself, so real structure inflates it: the threshold is conservative.
