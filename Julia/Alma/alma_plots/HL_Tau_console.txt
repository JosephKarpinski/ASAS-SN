5. RING WIDTHS, DUST TRAPPING AND RING SPACING
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A dust ring in a Gaussian gas pressure bump of width w has width w_d = w sqrt(alpha / (alpha + St)), i.e. alpha / St = 1 / ((w / w_d)^2 - 1) (Dullemond et al. 2018, DSHARP VI).
  w_d = Gaussian sigma of the fitted ring with the beam removed. A long-lived bump cannot be narrower than about one gas scale height H (passive-disk temperature, M* = 1.30 Msun), so w = H gives the LARGEST alpha / St the ring allows;
  a bump as wide as the distance to the nearest gap gives the smallest. A ring wider than H gives no limit: the bump could be as wide as the ring.

  alpha/St: ratio of the turbulent diffusion parameter to the Stokes number of the trapped grains (smaller = stronger trapping). The range in the 'St' columns is alpha divided by the two limits,
