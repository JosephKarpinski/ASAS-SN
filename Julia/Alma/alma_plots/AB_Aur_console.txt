  Geometry sensitivity of m=2 (first-order estimate, not a correction): an inclination error of 3 deg alone moves the deprojected radius by (cos i / cos(i + 3 deg) - 1)/2 x cos(2 phi), which on a profile with logarithmic slope S gives an m=2 of |S| times that:
           |          |  m=2 from a 3 deg error [%] |            measured m=2 [%]

  Each quantity is given relative to the ring amplitude used in the table above and as a fraction of the local azimuthal-mean flux. A measured m=2 of the same size as the estimate is not distinguishable from a small inclination error unless the ridge geometry fit (section 1b) or a second ring settles it.

  How to read this: a single significant positive arc covering a limited azimuth range at a ring radius (span of tens of degrees,

  The noise estimate for the arcs is the robust scatter of the smoothed residual itself, so real structure inflates it: the threshold is conservative.
