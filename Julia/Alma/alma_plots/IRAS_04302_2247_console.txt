  contrast tens of percent) is the signature of a dust trap such as a vortex. A +/- pair 180 deg apart (in the residual image, not the ring-averaged amplitudes)
  is what a small centre or inclination error produces.
  The noise estimate for the arcs is the robust scatter of the smoothed residual itself, so real structure inflates it: the threshold is conservative.
