  NOTE: i < 15 deg: inclination and PA are only weakly constrained by a nearly circular ring; trust the centre shift, not i and PA.
  Caveats: a flared, optically thick ring appears shifted along the minor axis (near side vs far side), which mimics a centre offset along that axis (section 1c tests it);
  formal errors exclude this. The asymmetry step applies the full (sub-pixel) centre shift to the residual image; the radial profile is recomputed about the nearest pixel (2.5 mas rounding).
  To adopt these values: physical_analysis(out; use_fitted_geometry = true), or pass incl_deg / pa_deg to run_alma_pipeline.
