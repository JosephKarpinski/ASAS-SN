A thin ridge is spread over all azimuths by the deprojection, so the radial profile is diluted toward the noise and any rings, gaps, Gaussian fits, temperatures and gap masses derived from it would be meaningless. The radial analysis was therefore skipped; the disc was tested on its aperture-integrated flux instead.
